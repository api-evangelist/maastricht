EDLAB Education Innovation Grants S.Brodin@maast… Fri, 10/09/2026 - 16:41 Do you have an idea that could improve teaching and learning at Maastricht University? The EDLAB Education Innovation Grants offer teaching staff from all UM faculties the opportunity to develop and test new approaches to education, with financial support from EDLAB. Whether it's a new teaching method, a different approach to assessment or a way to engage students more actively, the grants encourage experimentation and collaboration.
